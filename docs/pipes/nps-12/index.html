Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 12 pipe?

NPS 12 pipe has an outside diameter of 12.750 in (323.8 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 12 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

53.53 lb/ft (79.65 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.406 in (10.3 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 12?

DN 300. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 300 pipe does not measure 300 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 323.8 mm.
