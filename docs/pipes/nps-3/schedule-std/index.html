NPS 3 Standard (STD) Pipe

3.500 in (88.9 mm) outside diameter on a 0.216 in (5.5 mm) wall, giving a 3.068 in (77.9 mm) bore and 7.58 lb/ft (11.27 kg/m) empty. ASME B36.10M.

Flanges for NPS 3 pipe

Flange dimensions are set by NPS and pressure class, not by schedule, so every flange below fits this pipe. The exception is the weld neck: its bore is machined to the pipe it is welded to, which for Standard (STD) means 3.068 in (77.9 mm). Order the flange to the schedule or the bores will not line up at the weld.

Common questions

What is the inside diameter of NPS 3 standard (std) pipe?

3.068 in (77.9 mm). The outside diameter is fixed at 3.500 in (88.9 mm) by ASME B36.10M, and the 0.216 in (5.5 mm) wall takes 0.432 in off it — 0.216 in from each side — leaving that bore.

How much does NPS 3 standard (std) pipe weigh?

7.58 lb/ft (11.27 kg/m) empty, as plain-end carbon steel. Full of water it comes to 10.78 lb/ft (16.04 kg/m), because the bore holds 0.384 US gallons per foot. One short ton of this pipe is about 264 ft.

Does NPS 3 standard (std) pipe use special flanges or fittings?

No. Flanges and buttweld fittings are dimensioned from NPS, so NPS 3 hardware fits this pipe in any schedule. The one thing that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange or a fitting end prep, which has to be 3.068 in (77.9 mm) to match.
