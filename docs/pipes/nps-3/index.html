Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 3 pipe?

NPS 3 pipe has an outside diameter of 3.500 in (88.9 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 3 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

7.58 lb/ft (11.27 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.216 in (5.5 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 3?

DN 80. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 80 pipe does not measure 80 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 88.9 mm.
