What NPS 18 is

NPS 18 pipe measures 18.000 in (457.2 mm) across the outside, so here the size designation and the outside diameter in inches are the same number. That holds from NPS 14 upward and for no size below it. The metric designator is DN 450, which is likewise a label and not a measurement in millimetres. Whatever the schedule, the outside diameter stays at 18.000 in (457.2 mm): a heavier wall grows inward and takes the difference out of the bore.

Reading the table

B36.10M lists 13 designations in NPS 18, which between them give 13 different wall thicknesses. The lightest is Schedule 5 at 0.165 in (4.2 mm) and the heaviest is Schedule 160 at 1.781 in (45.2 mm). Going from one to the other takes the bore from 17.670 in (448.8 mm) down to 14.438 in (366.7 mm), which cuts the flow area by 33% and raises the weight from 31.43 to 308.51 lb/ft, a factor of 9.8.

Standard weight does not coincide with any numbered schedule in this size. Extra strong does not coincide with a numbered schedule here. Where two designations share a wall they are the same pipe under two names, and either may appear on a mill certificate. The match is specific to the size, so it is the wall thickness in inches that should go on a requisition, not the designation alone.

For handling and support loads, a 20 ft length of standard weight NPS 18 pipe weighs about 1412 lb (640 kg) empty and 3438 lb (1560 kg) full of water. The water-filled figure is the one that governs pipe support spacing and the load during a hydrostatic test, including on lines that will carry gas or vapour in service.

Flow and pressure capacity by schedule

Two questions decide a schedule: how much the bore will carry, and how much pressure the wall will hold. The flow columns give the water flow through each bore at three velocities that bracket ordinary liquid service, from a pump suction line at the low end to a pump discharge line at the high end. The pressure columns are the ASME B31.3 pressure design equation solved for pressure, first on the nominal wall and then on the wall after the 12.5% mill under-tolerance that seamless pipe is allowed.

Flanges for NPS 18 pipe

ASME B16.5 sizes a flange from the NPS and the pressure class, never from the schedule, so every flange below fits every wall in the table above. In NPS 18 a Class 150 flange is 25.00 in (635.0 mm) across and 1.56 in (39.6 mm) thick, held by 16 bolts of 1 1/8 in. A Class 1500 flange for the same pipe is 36.00 in (914.4 mm) across and 6.38 in (162.1 mm) thick, on 16 bolts of 2 3/4 in. Because the bolt circle moves with the class, flanges of two different classes will not bolt together even though both fit the pipe.

The one flange dimension that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange, which is machined to the inside diameter of the pipe it is welded to. A weld neck flange for standard weight NPS 18 pipe is bored 17.250 in (438.1 mm); ordered for Schedule 160 it is bored 14.438 in (366.7 mm). The schedule therefore belongs on every weld neck flange requisition.

Buttweld fittings for NPS 18 pipe

ASME B16.9 sets the dimensions of a buttweld fitting from the NPS alone, so the figures below hold for every schedule in the size. A long radius 90° elbow in NPS 18 measures 27.00 in (685.8 mm) from its centre to each end, which is the figure a piping layout adds for every change of direction. The fitting is still ordered to a schedule, because its wall has to match the pipe at the weld.

Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 18 pipe?

NPS 18 pipe has an outside diameter of 18.000 in (457.2 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

What is the inside diameter of NPS 18 schedule 40 pipe?

16.876 in (428.7 mm). The 0.562 in (14.3 mm) wall comes off the 18.000 in (457.2 mm) outside diameter twice, once on each side, which leaves that bore and a flow area of 223.68 in².

How much does NPS 18 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

104.67 lb/ft (155.76 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.562 in (14.3 mm). It holds 11.620 US gallons of water per foot.

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 18?

DN 450. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 450 pipe does not measure 450 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 457.2 mm.
