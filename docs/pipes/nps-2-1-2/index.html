What NPS 2 1/2 is

NPS 2 1/2 pipe measures 2.875 in (73.0 mm) across the outside, which is 0.375 in more than the 2 1/2 in its name suggests. Below NPS 14 the designation is a label inherited from iron pipe sizing and matches neither the outside diameter nor the bore of any schedule exactly. The metric designator is DN 65, which is likewise a label and not a measurement in millimetres. Whatever the schedule, the outside diameter stays at 2.875 in (73.0 mm): a heavier wall grows inward and takes the difference out of the bore.

Common in building services and fire protection, where it is the usual hose connection size, and uncommon in process plants, many of which step from NPS 2 to NPS 3.

Reading the table

B36.10 lists 9 designations in NPS 2 1/2, which between them give 7 different wall thicknesses. The lightest is Schedule 5 at 0.083 in (2.1 mm) and the heaviest is Double Extra Strong (XXS) at 0.552 in (14.0 mm). Going from one to the other takes the bore from 2.709 in (68.8 mm) down to 1.771 in (45.0 mm), which cuts the flow area by 57% and raises the weight from 2.48 to 13.71 lb/ft, a factor of 5.5.

Standard weight equals Schedule 40 in this size. Extra strong equals Schedule 80. Where two designations share a wall they are the same pipe under two names, and either may appear on a mill certificate. The match is specific to the size, so it is the wall thickness in inches that should go on a requisition, not the designation alone.

For handling and support loads, a 20 ft length of standard weight NPS 2 1/2 pipe weighs about 116 lb (53 kg) empty and 157 lb (71 kg) full of water. The water-filled figure is the one that governs pipe support spacing and the load during a hydrostatic test, including on lines that will carry gas or vapour in service.

Flow and pressure capacity by schedule

Two questions decide a schedule: how much the bore will carry, and how much pressure the wall will hold. The flow columns give the water flow through each bore at three velocities that bracket ordinary liquid service, from a pump suction line at the low end to a pump discharge line at the high end. The pressure columns are the ASME B31.3 pressure design equation solved for pressure, first on the nominal wall and then on the wall after the 12.5% mill under-tolerance that seamless pipe is allowed.

Flanges for NPS 2 1/2 pipe

ASME B16.5 sizes a flange from the NPS and the pressure class, never from the schedule, so every flange below fits every wall in the table above. In NPS 2 1/2 a Class 150 flange is 7.00 in (177.8 mm) across and 0.81 in (20.6 mm) thick, held by 4 bolts of 5/8 in. A Class 2500 flange for the same pipe is 10.50 in (266.7 mm) across and 2.25 in (57.1 mm) thick, on 8 bolts of 1 1/8 in. Because the bolt circle moves with the class, flanges of two different classes will not bolt together even though both fit the pipe.

The one flange dimension that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange, which is machined to the inside diameter of the pipe it is welded to. A weld neck flange for standard weight NPS 2 1/2 pipe is bored 2.469 in (62.7 mm); ordered for Double Extra Strong (XXS) it is bored 1.771 in (45.0 mm). The schedule therefore belongs on every weld neck flange requisition.

Buttweld fittings for NPS 2 1/2 pipe

ASME B16.9 sets the dimensions of a buttweld fitting from the NPS alone, so the figures below hold for every schedule in the size. A long radius 90° elbow in NPS 2 1/2 measures 3.75 in (95.2 mm) from its centre to each end, which is the figure a piping layout adds for every change of direction. The fitting is still ordered to a schedule, because its wall has to match the pipe at the weld.

Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 2 1/2 pipe?

NPS 2 1/2 pipe has an outside diameter of 2.875 in (73.0 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

What is the inside diameter of NPS 2 1/2 schedule 40 pipe?

2.469 in (62.7 mm). The 0.203 in (5.2 mm) wall comes off the 2.875 in (73.0 mm) outside diameter twice, once on each side, which leaves that bore and a flow area of 4.79 in².

How much does NPS 2 1/2 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

5.80 lb/ft (8.63 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.203 in (5.2 mm). It holds 0.249 US gallons of water per foot.

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 2 1/2?

DN 65. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 65 pipe does not measure 65 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 73.0 mm.
