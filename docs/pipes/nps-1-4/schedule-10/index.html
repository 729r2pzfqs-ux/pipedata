Common questions

What is the inside diameter of NPS 1/4 schedule 10 pipe?

0.410 in (10.4 mm). The outside diameter is fixed at 0.540 in (13.7 mm) by ASME B36.10M, and the 0.065 in (1.7 mm) wall takes 0.130 in off it — 0.065 in from each side — leaving that bore.

How much does NPS 1/4 schedule 10 pipe weigh?

0.33 lb/ft (0.49 kg/m) empty, as plain-end carbon steel. Full of water it comes to 0.39 lb/ft (0.58 kg/m), because the bore holds 0.007 US gallons per foot. One short ton of this pipe is about 6065 ft.

Does NPS 1/4 schedule 10 pipe use special flanges or fittings?

No. Flanges and buttweld fittings are dimensioned from NPS, so NPS 1/4 hardware fits this pipe in any schedule. The one thing that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange or a fitting end prep, which has to be 0.410 in (10.4 mm) to match.
