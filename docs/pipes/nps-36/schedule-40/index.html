Common questions

What is the inside diameter of NPS 36 schedule 40 pipe?

34.500 in (876.3 mm). The outside diameter is fixed at 36.000 in (914.4 mm) by ASME B36.10M, and the 0.750 in (19.0 mm) wall takes 1.500 in off it — 0.750 in from each side — leaving that bore.

How much does NPS 36 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

282.36 lb/ft (420.19 kg/m) empty, as plain-end carbon steel. Full of water it comes to 687.63 lb/ft (1023.30 kg/m), because the bore holds 48.562 US gallons per foot. One short ton of this pipe is about 7 ft.

Does NPS 36 schedule 40 pipe use special flanges or fittings?

No. Flanges and buttweld fittings are dimensioned from NPS, so NPS 36 hardware fits this pipe in any schedule. The one thing that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange or a fitting end prep, which has to be 34.500 in (876.3 mm) to match.
