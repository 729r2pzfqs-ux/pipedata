Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 3/4 pipe?

NPS 3/4 pipe has an outside diameter of 1.050 in (26.7 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 3/4 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

1.13 lb/ft (1.68 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.113 in (2.9 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 3/4?

DN 20. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 20 pipe does not measure 20 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 26.7 mm.
