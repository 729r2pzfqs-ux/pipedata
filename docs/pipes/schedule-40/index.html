Common questions

What sizes is schedule 40 pipe made in?

ASME B36.10M publishes schedule 40 in 30 sizes, from NPS 1/8 through NPS 36. Wall thickness runs from 0.068 in (1.7 mm) at the small end to 0.750 in (19.0 mm) at the large end.

Does schedule 40 mean the same wall thickness in every size?

No. A schedule number is a series, not a thickness. It sets a wall that rises with the pipe size so the pressure rating stays roughly constant across the range — which is the whole point of the schedule system.
