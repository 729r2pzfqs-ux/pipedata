What NPS 22 is

NPS 22 pipe measures 22.000 in (558.8 mm) across the outside, so here the size designation and the outside diameter in inches are the same number. That holds from NPS 14 upward and for no size below it. The metric designator is DN 550, which is likewise a label and not a measurement in millimetres. Whatever the schedule, the outside diameter stays at 22.000 in (558.8 mm): a heavier wall grows inward and takes the difference out of the bore.

Less commonly stocked than the sizes either side of it; many piping size lists step from NPS 20 to NPS 24. B36.10M publishes no Schedule 40 wall for it.

Reading the table

B36.10 lists 12 designations in NPS 22, which between them give 10 different wall thicknesses. The lightest is Schedule 5 at 0.188 in (4.8 mm) and the heaviest is Schedule 160 at 2.125 in (54.0 mm). Going from one to the other takes the bore from 21.624 in (549.2 mm) down to 17.750 in (450.8 mm), which cuts the flow area by 33% and raises the weight from 43.84 to 451.49 lb/ft, a factor of 10.3.

Standard weight equals Schedule 20 in this size. Extra strong equals Schedule 30. Where two designations share a wall they are the same pipe under two names, and either may appear on a mill certificate. The match is specific to the size, so it is the wall thickness in inches that should go on a requisition, not the designation alone.

For handling and support loads, a 20 ft length of standard weight NPS 22 pipe weighs about 1734 lb (786 kg) empty and 4809 lb (2181 kg) full of water. The water-filled figure is the one that governs pipe support spacing and the load during a hydrostatic test, including on lines that will carry gas or vapour in service.

Flow and pressure capacity by schedule

Two questions decide a schedule: how much the bore will carry, and how much pressure the wall will hold. The flow columns give the water flow through each bore at three velocities that bracket ordinary liquid service, from a pump suction line at the low end to a pump discharge line at the high end. The pressure columns are the ASME B31.3 pressure design equation solved for pressure, first on the nominal wall and then on the wall after the 12.5% mill under-tolerance that seamless pipe is allowed.

Flanges for NPS 22 pipe

ASME B16.5 publishes no flange in NPS 22 and ASME B16.9 no buttweld fitting. That is the practical meaning of an obsolete size: pipe of this diameter exists in old plant, but nothing standard is made to connect to it. New work uses the next size up or down.

Buttweld fittings for NPS 22 pipe

ASME B16.9 sets the dimensions of a buttweld fitting from the NPS alone, so the figures below hold for every schedule in the size. A long radius 90° elbow in NPS 22 measures 33.00 in (838.2 mm) from its centre to each end, which is the figure a piping layout adds for every change of direction. The fitting is still ordered to a schedule, because its wall has to match the pipe at the weld.

Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 22 pipe?

NPS 22 pipe has an outside diameter of 22.000 in (558.8 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

What is the inside diameter of NPS 22 standard weight pipe?

21.250 in (539.8 mm). The 0.375 in (9.5 mm) wall comes off the 22.000 in (558.8 mm) outside diameter twice, once on each side, which leaves that bore and a flow area of 354.66 in².

How much does NPS 22 standard weight pipe weigh?

86.69 lb/ft (129.01 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.375 in (9.5 mm). It holds 18.424 US gallons of water per foot.

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 22?

DN 550. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 550 pipe does not measure 550 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 558.8 mm.
