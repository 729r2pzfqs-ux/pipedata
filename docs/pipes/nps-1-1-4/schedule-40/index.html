NPS 1 1/4 Schedule 40 Pipe

Flanges for NPS 1 1/4 pipe

Flange dimensions are set by NPS and pressure class, not by schedule, so every flange below fits this pipe. The exception is the weld neck: its bore is machined to the pipe it is welded to, which for Schedule 40 means 1.380 in (35.1 mm). Order the flange to the schedule or the bores will not line up at the weld.

Common questions

What is the inside diameter of NPS 1 1/4 schedule 40 pipe?

1.380 in (35.1 mm). The outside diameter is fixed at 1.660 in (42.2 mm) by ASME B36.10M, and the 0.140 in (3.6 mm) wall takes 0.280 in off it — 0.140 in from each side — leaving that bore.

How much does NPS 1 1/4 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

2.27 lb/ft (3.38 kg/m) empty, as plain-end carbon steel. Full of water it comes to 2.92 lb/ft (4.35 kg/m), because the bore holds 0.078 US gallons per foot. One short ton of this pipe is about 880 ft.

Does NPS 1 1/4 schedule 40 pipe use special flanges or fittings?

No. Flanges and buttweld fittings are dimensioned from NPS, so NPS 1 1/4 hardware fits this pipe in any schedule. The one thing that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange or a fitting end prep, which has to be 1.380 in (35.1 mm) to match.
