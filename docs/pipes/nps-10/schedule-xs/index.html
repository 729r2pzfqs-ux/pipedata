NPS 10 Extra Strong (XS) Pipe

10.750 in (273.1 mm) outside diameter on a 0.500 in (12.7 mm) wall, giving a 9.750 in (247.6 mm) bore and 54.74 lb/ft (81.46 kg/m) empty. ASME B36.10M.

Flanges for NPS 10 pipe

Flange dimensions are set by NPS and pressure class, not by schedule, so every flange below fits this pipe. The exception is the weld neck: its bore is machined to the pipe it is welded to, which for Extra Strong (XS) means 9.750 in (247.6 mm). Order the flange to the schedule or the bores will not line up at the weld.

Common questions

What is the inside diameter of NPS 10 extra strong (xs) pipe?

9.750 in (247.6 mm). The outside diameter is fixed at 10.750 in (273.1 mm) by ASME B36.10M, and the 0.500 in (12.7 mm) wall takes 1.000 in off it — 0.500 in from each side — leaving that bore.

How much does NPS 10 extra strong (xs) pipe weigh?

54.74 lb/ft (81.46 kg/m) empty, as plain-end carbon steel. Full of water it comes to 87.10 lb/ft (129.62 kg/m), because the bore holds 3.879 US gallons per foot. One short ton of this pipe is about 37 ft.

Does NPS 10 extra strong (xs) pipe use special flanges or fittings?

No. Flanges and buttweld fittings are dimensioned from NPS, so NPS 10 hardware fits this pipe in any schedule. The one thing that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange or a fitting end prep, which has to be 9.750 in (247.6 mm) to match.
