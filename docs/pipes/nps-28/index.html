What NPS 28 is

NPS 28 pipe measures 28.000 in (711.2 mm) across the outside, so here the size designation and the outside diameter in inches are the same number. That holds from NPS 14 upward and for no size below it. The metric designator is DN 700, which is likewise a label and not a measurement in millimetres. Whatever the schedule, the outside diameter stays at 28.000 in (711.2 mm): a heavier wall grows inward and takes the difference out of the bore.

A large-diameter size, typically supplied as welded pipe, with flanges to ASME B16.47.

Inside diameter is OD − 2t. Weight is calculated for carbon steel as w = 10.69 × t × (OD − t) lb/ft and is the plain-end weight, excluding coatings, linings and fittings. Water-filled weight adds fresh water at 8.3454 lb per US gallon.

Reading the table

B36.10 lists 5 designations in NPS 28, which between them give 4 different wall thicknesses. The lightest is Schedule 10 at 0.312 in (7.9 mm) and the heaviest is Schedule 30 at 0.625 in (15.9 mm). Going from one to the other takes the bore from 27.376 in (695.4 mm) down to 26.750 in (679.4 mm), which cuts the flow area by 5% and raises the weight from 92.35 to 182.90 lb/ft, a factor of 2.0.

Standard weight does not coincide with any numbered schedule in this size. Extra strong equals Schedule 20. Where two designations share a wall they are the same pipe under two names, and either may appear on a mill certificate. The match is specific to the size, so it is the wall thickness in inches that should go on a requisition, not the designation alone.

For handling and support loads, a 20 ft length of standard weight NPS 28 pipe weighs about 2215 lb (1005 kg) empty and 7272 lb (3298 kg) full of water. The water-filled figure is the one that governs pipe support spacing and the load during a hydrostatic test, including on lines that will carry gas or vapour in service.

Flow and pressure capacity by schedule

Two questions decide a schedule: how much the bore will carry, and how much pressure the wall will hold. The flow columns give the water flow through each bore at three velocities that bracket ordinary liquid service, from a pump suction line at the low end to a pump discharge line at the high end. The pressure columns are the ASME B31.3 pressure design equation solved for pressure, first on the nominal wall and then on the wall after the 12.5% mill under-tolerance that seamless pipe is allowed.

Flanges for NPS 28 pipe

ASME B16.5 stops at NPS 24, so there is no B16.5 flange in this size. Flanges for NPS 28 pipe come from ASME B16.47, which publishes two series, A and B, that do not bolt to each other. The series has to be settled before anything else about the flange is. See large diameter flanges.

Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 28 pipe?

NPS 28 pipe has an outside diameter of 28.000 in (711.2 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

What is the inside diameter of NPS 28 standard weight pipe?

27.250 in (692.1 mm). The 0.375 in (9.5 mm) wall comes off the 28.000 in (711.2 mm) outside diameter twice, once on each side, which leaves that bore and a flow area of 583.21 in².

How much does NPS 28 standard weight pipe weigh?

110.74 lb/ft (164.80 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.375 in (9.5 mm). It holds 30.296 US gallons of water per foot.

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 28?

DN 700. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 700 pipe does not measure 700 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 711.2 mm.
