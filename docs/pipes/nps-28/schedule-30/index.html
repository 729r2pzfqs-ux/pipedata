Common questions

What is the inside diameter of NPS 28 schedule 30 pipe?

26.750 in (679.4 mm). The outside diameter is fixed at 28.000 in (711.2 mm) by ASME B36.10M, and the 0.625 in (15.9 mm) wall takes 1.250 in off it — 0.625 in from each side — leaving that bore.

How much does NPS 28 schedule 30 pipe weigh?

182.73 lb/ft (271.93 kg/m) empty, as plain-end carbon steel. Full of water it comes to 426.37 lb/ft (634.51 kg/m), because the bore holds 29.195 US gallons per foot. One short ton of this pipe is about 11 ft.

Does NPS 28 schedule 30 pipe use special flanges or fittings?

No. Flanges and buttweld fittings are dimensioned from NPS, so NPS 28 hardware fits this pipe in any schedule. The one thing that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange or a fitting end prep, which has to be 26.750 in (679.4 mm) to match.
