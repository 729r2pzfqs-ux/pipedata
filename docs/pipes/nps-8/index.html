Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 8 pipe?

NPS 8 pipe has an outside diameter of 8.625 in (219.1 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 8 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

28.55 lb/ft (42.49 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.322 in (8.2 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 8?

DN 200. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 200 pipe does not measure 200 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 219.1 mm.
