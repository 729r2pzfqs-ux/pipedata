Common questions

What is the inside diameter of NPS 4 1/2 extra strong (xs) pipe?

4.290 in (109.0 mm). The outside diameter is fixed at 5.000 in (127.0 mm) by ASME B36.10M, and the 0.355 in (9.0 mm) wall takes 0.710 in off it — 0.355 in from each side — leaving that bore.

How much does NPS 4 1/2 extra strong (xs) pipe weigh?

17.61 lb/ft (26.21 kg/m) empty, as plain-end carbon steel. Full of water it comes to 23.88 lb/ft (35.53 kg/m), because the bore holds 0.751 US gallons per foot. One short ton of this pipe is about 114 ft.

Does NPS 4 1/2 extra strong (xs) pipe use special flanges or fittings?

No. Flanges and buttweld fittings are dimensioned from NPS, so NPS 4 1/2 hardware fits this pipe in any schedule. The one thing that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange or a fitting end prep, which has to be 4.290 in (109.0 mm) to match.
