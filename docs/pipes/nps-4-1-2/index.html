Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 4 1/2 pipe?

NPS 4 1/2 pipe has an outside diameter of 5.000 in (127.0 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 4 1/2 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

12.54 lb/ft (18.66 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.247 in (6.3 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 4 1/2?

DN 115. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 115 pipe does not measure 115 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 127.0 mm.
