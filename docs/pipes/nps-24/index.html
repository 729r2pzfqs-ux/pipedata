Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 24 pipe?

NPS 24 pipe has an outside diameter of 24.000 in (609.6 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 24 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

171.30 lb/ft (254.92 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.688 in (17.5 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 24?

DN 600. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 600 pipe does not measure 600 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 609.6 mm.
