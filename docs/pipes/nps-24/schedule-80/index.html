Flanges for NPS 24 pipe

Flange dimensions are set by NPS and pressure class, not by schedule, so every flange below fits this pipe. The exception is the weld neck: its bore is machined to the pipe it is welded to, which for Schedule 80 means 21.562 in (547.7 mm). Order the flange to the schedule or the bores will not line up at the weld.

Common questions

What is the inside diameter of NPS 24 schedule 80 pipe?

21.562 in (547.7 mm). The outside diameter is fixed at 24.000 in (609.6 mm) by ASME B36.10M, and the 1.219 in (31.0 mm) wall takes 2.438 in off it — 1.219 in from each side — leaving that bore.

How much does NPS 24 schedule 80 pipe weigh?

296.59 lb/ft (441.37 kg/m) empty, as plain-end carbon steel. Full of water it comes to 454.89 lb/ft (676.95 kg/m), because the bore holds 18.969 US gallons per foot. One short ton of this pipe is about 7 ft.

Does NPS 24 schedule 80 pipe use special flanges or fittings?

No. Flanges and buttweld fittings are dimensioned from NPS, so NPS 24 hardware fits this pipe in any schedule. The one thing that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange or a fitting end prep, which has to be 21.562 in (547.7 mm) to match.
