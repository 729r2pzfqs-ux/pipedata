Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 30 pipe?

NPS 30 pipe has an outside diameter of 30.000 in (762.0 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 30 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

118.65 lb/ft (176.57 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.375 in (9.5 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 30?

DN 750. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 750 pipe does not measure 750 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 762.0 mm.
