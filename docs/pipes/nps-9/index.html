Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 9 pipe?

NPS 9 pipe has an outside diameter of 9.625 in (244.5 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 9 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

33.91 lb/ft (50.46 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.342 in (8.7 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 9?

DN 225. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 225 pipe does not measure 225 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 244.5 mm.
