Flanges for NPS 20 pipe

Flange dimensions are set by NPS and pressure class, not by schedule, so every flange below fits this pipe. The exception is the weld neck: its bore is machined to the pipe it is welded to, which for Schedule 160 means 16.062 in (408.0 mm). Order the flange to the schedule or the bores will not line up at the weld.

Common questions

What is the inside diameter of NPS 20 schedule 160 pipe?

16.062 in (408.0 mm). The outside diameter is fixed at 20.000 in (508.0 mm) by ASME B36.10M, and the 1.969 in (50.0 mm) wall takes 3.938 in off it — 1.969 in from each side — leaving that bore.

How much does NPS 20 schedule 160 pipe weigh?

379.18 lb/ft (564.28 kg/m) empty, as plain-end carbon steel. Full of water it comes to 467.02 lb/ft (695.00 kg/m), because the bore holds 10.526 US gallons per foot. One short ton of this pipe is about 5 ft.

Does NPS 20 schedule 160 pipe use special flanges or fittings?

No. Flanges and buttweld fittings are dimensioned from NPS, so NPS 20 hardware fits this pipe in any schedule. The one thing that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange or a fitting end prep, which has to be 16.062 in (408.0 mm) to match.
