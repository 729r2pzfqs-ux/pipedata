Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 20 pipe?

NPS 20 pipe has an outside diameter of 20.000 in (508.0 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 20 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

123.11 lb/ft (183.21 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.594 in (15.1 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 20?

DN 500. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 500 pipe does not measure 500 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 508.0 mm.
