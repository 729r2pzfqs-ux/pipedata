Flanges for NPS 16 pipe

Flange dimensions are set by NPS and pressure class, not by schedule, so every flange below fits this pipe. The exception is the weld neck: its bore is machined to the pipe it is welded to, which for Schedule 140 means 13.124 in (333.3 mm). Order the flange to the schedule or the bores will not line up at the weld.

Common questions

What is the inside diameter of NPS 16 schedule 140 pipe?

13.124 in (333.3 mm). The outside diameter is fixed at 16.000 in (406.4 mm) by ASME B36.10M, and the 1.438 in (36.5 mm) wall takes 2.876 in off it — 1.438 in from each side — leaving that bore.

How much does NPS 16 schedule 140 pipe weigh?

223.65 lb/ft (332.82 kg/m) empty, as plain-end carbon steel. Full of water it comes to 282.29 lb/ft (420.09 kg/m), because the bore holds 7.027 US gallons per foot. One short ton of this pipe is about 9 ft.

Does NPS 16 schedule 140 pipe use special flanges or fittings?

No. Flanges and buttweld fittings are dimensioned from NPS, so NPS 16 hardware fits this pipe in any schedule. The one thing that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange or a fitting end prep, which has to be 13.124 in (333.3 mm) to match.
