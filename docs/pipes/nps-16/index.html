Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 16 pipe?

NPS 16 pipe has an outside diameter of 16.000 in (406.4 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 16 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

82.77 lb/ft (123.18 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.500 in (12.7 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 16?

DN 400. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 400 pipe does not measure 400 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 406.4 mm.
