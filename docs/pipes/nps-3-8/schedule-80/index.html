Common questions

What is the inside diameter of NPS 3/8 schedule 80 pipe?

0.423 in (10.7 mm). The outside diameter is fixed at 0.675 in (17.1 mm) by ASME B36.10M, and the 0.126 in (3.2 mm) wall takes 0.252 in off it — 0.126 in from each side — leaving that bore.

How much does NPS 3/8 schedule 80 pipe weigh?

0.74 lb/ft (1.10 kg/m) empty, as plain-end carbon steel. Full of water it comes to 0.80 lb/ft (1.19 kg/m), because the bore holds 0.007 US gallons per foot. One short ton of this pipe is about 2707 ft.

Does NPS 3/8 schedule 80 pipe use special flanges or fittings?

No. Flanges and buttweld fittings are dimensioned from NPS, so NPS 3/8 hardware fits this pipe in any schedule. The one thing that does follow the schedule is the bore of a weld neck flange or a fitting end prep, which has to be 0.423 in (10.7 mm) to match.
