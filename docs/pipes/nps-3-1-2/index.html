Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 3 1/2 pipe?

NPS 3 1/2 pipe has an outside diameter of 4.000 in (101.6 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 3 1/2 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

9.11 lb/ft (13.56 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.226 in (5.7 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 3 1/2?

DN 90. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 90 pipe does not measure 90 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 101.6 mm.
