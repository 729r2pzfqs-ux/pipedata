Common questions

What is the outside diameter of NPS 6 pipe?

NPS 6 pipe has an outside diameter of 6.625 in (168.3 mm). That outside diameter is fixed: it is identical in every schedule, and only the wall thickness — and therefore the bore — changes from one schedule to the next.

How much does NPS 6 schedule 40 pipe weigh?

18.97 lb/ft (28.24 kg/m) as plain-end carbon steel, on a wall thickness of 0.280 in (7.1 mm).

What is the metric equivalent of NPS 6?

DN 150. DN is a dimensionless designator, not a measurement — DN 150 pipe does not measure 150 mm anywhere. Its actual outside diameter is 168.3 mm.
