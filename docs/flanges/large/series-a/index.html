ASME B16.47 Series A Flanges

Series A is the heavier of the two large-flange series and the default choice for new refinery, power and pipeline work in North America. It has a larger outside diameter, a thicker flange and fewer, larger bolts than Series B at the same size and class.

Common questions

What is ASME B16.47 Series A?

Series A is the heavier of the two large-flange series and the default choice for new refinery, power and pipeline work in North America. It has a larger outside diameter, a thicker flange and fewer, larger bolts than Series B at the same size and class.

What classes does Series A come in?

Class 150, Class 300, Class 400, Class 600 and Class 900, in sizes nps 26 through nps 60. Series B carries Class 75, Class 150, Class 300, Class 400, Class 600 and Class 900 instead, which is one reason the two are easy to confuse on a requisition.
