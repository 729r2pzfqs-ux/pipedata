Common questions

How many bolts does an NPS 36 Series A Class 150 flange take?

32 bolts of 1 1/2 in diameter, on a 42.75 in (1085.8 mm) bolt circle.

Are these dimensions the same as Series B?

No. Series A and Series B differ in outside diameter, bolt circle and bolt count at every size. The two will not bolt together, and a gasket for one will not suit the other.
