NPS 2 1/2 Class 2500 Blind Flange

ASME B16.5 dimensions for one size and class: 10.50 in (266.7 mm) outside diameter, 2.25 in (57.1 mm) thick, on a 7.75 in (196.8 mm) bolt circle with 8 × 1 1/8 in bolts.

Weight and end load

A blind flange has no bore, so its outside diameter and thickness are the whole of its dimensional definition — and its weight and the load it carries are the two things people actually need. Both are calculated here from the B16.5 figures above.

Derived figures for the NPS 2 1/2 Class 2500 blind flange.
QuantityNPS 2 1/2 Class 2500
Approximate weight55.3 lb (25.1 kg)
End thrust at rated pressure82256 lbf (365.9 kN)
Load per bolt10282 lbf

Weight is a solid-disc estimate from the outside diameter and the minimum thickness at 0.2836 lb/in³, and ignores the raised face, so it runs slightly light against a real forging. End thrust is the rated pressure acting on the raised face area; it is not a bolt-load calculation, which must also cover gasket seating.

Common questions

What are the bolt holes on an NPS 2 1/2 Class 2500 flange?

8 holes of 1.250 in diameter on a 7.75 in bolt circle, taking 1 1/8 in bolts. B16.5 holes are 1/8 in larger than the nominal bolt. They straddle the vertical and horizontal centrelines rather than sitting on them, so hole spacing round the circle is 3.04 in.

What pressure is an NPS 2 1/2 Class 2500 flange good for?

6170 psig (425.4 bar) at 100 °F in A105 carbon steel, falling as metal temperature rises. Class 2500 is a designation, not a pressure — see the P-T tables for your material and temperature.

Is a Class 2500 blind flange interchangeable with another type?

At the bolted joint, yes: every B16.5 type in NPS 2 1/2 Class 2500 shares the 10.50 in outside diameter, 7.75 in bolt circle and 8 × 1 1/8 in bolting, so any two will mate. What is not interchangeable is how they join the pipe, and therefore what service they suit.
