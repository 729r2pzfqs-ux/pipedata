NPS 10 Class 2500 Weld Neck Flange

ASME B16.5 dimensions for one size and class: 26.50 in (673.1 mm) outside diameter, 6.50 in (165.1 mm) thick, on a 21.25 in (539.8 mm) bolt circle with 12 × 2 1/2 in bolts.

Bore: order the flange to the schedule

A weld neck flange is bored to match the pipe it is butt-welded to, so NPS 10 Class 2500 is not one part number but 14 — one for each schedule B36.10M publishes in this size. Everything in the table above is common to all of them; the bore below is not. Specifying “NPS 10 Class 2500 weld neck” without the schedule is an incomplete requisition.

Common questions

What are the bolt holes on an NPS 10 Class 2500 flange?

12 holes of 2.625 in diameter on a 21.25 in bolt circle, taking 2 1/2 in bolts. B16.5 holes are 1/8 in larger than the nominal bolt. They straddle the vertical and horizontal centrelines rather than sitting on them, so hole spacing round the circle is 5.56 in.

What pressure is an NPS 10 Class 2500 flange good for?

6170 psig (425.4 bar) at 100 °F in A105 carbon steel, falling as metal temperature rises. Class 2500 is a designation, not a pressure — see the P-T tables for your material and temperature.

Is a Class 2500 weld neck flange interchangeable with another type?

At the bolted joint, yes: every B16.5 type in NPS 10 Class 2500 shares the 26.50 in outside diameter, 21.25 in bolt circle and 12 × 2 1/2 in bolting, so any two will mate. What is not interchangeable is how they join the pipe, and therefore what service they suit.
