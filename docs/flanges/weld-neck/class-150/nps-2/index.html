NPS 2 Class 150 Weld Neck Flange

ASME B16.5 dimensions for one size and class: 6.00 in (152.4 mm) outside diameter, 0.75 in (19.0 mm) thick, on a 4.75 in (120.6 mm) bolt circle with 4 × 5/8 in bolts.

Bore: order the flange to the schedule

A weld neck flange is bored to match the pipe it is butt-welded to, so NPS 2 Class 150 is not one part number but 8 — one for each schedule B36.10M publishes in this size. Everything in the table above is common to all of them; the bore below is not. Specifying “NPS 2 Class 150 weld neck” without the schedule is an incomplete requisition.

Common questions

What are the bolt holes on an NPS 2 Class 150 flange?

4 holes of 0.750 in diameter on a 4.75 in bolt circle, taking 5/8 in bolts. B16.5 holes are 1/8 in larger than the nominal bolt. They straddle the vertical and horizontal centrelines rather than sitting on them, so hole spacing round the circle is 3.73 in.

What pressure is an NPS 2 Class 150 flange good for?

285 psig (19.7 bar) at 100 °F in A105 carbon steel, falling as metal temperature rises. Class 150 is a designation, not a pressure — see the P-T tables for your material and temperature.

Is a Class 150 weld neck flange interchangeable with another type?

At the bolted joint, yes: every B16.5 type in NPS 2 Class 150 shares the 6.00 in outside diameter, 4.75 in bolt circle and 4 × 5/8 in bolting, so any two will mate. What is not interchangeable is how they join the pipe, and therefore what service they suit.
