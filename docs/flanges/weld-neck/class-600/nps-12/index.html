NPS 12 Class 600 Weld Neck Flange

ASME B16.5 dimensions for one size and class: 22.00 in (558.8 mm) outside diameter, 2.62 in (66.5 mm) thick, on a 19.25 in (488.9 mm) bolt circle with 20 × 1 1/4 in bolts.

Bore: order the flange to the schedule

A weld neck flange is bored to match the pipe it is butt-welded to, so NPS 12 Class 600 is not one part number but 14 — one for each schedule B36.10M publishes in this size. Everything in the table above is common to all of them; the bore below is not. Specifying “NPS 12 Class 600 weld neck” without the schedule is an incomplete requisition.

Common questions

What are the bolt holes on an NPS 12 Class 600 flange?

20 holes of 1.375 in diameter on a 19.25 in bolt circle, taking 1 1/4 in bolts. B16.5 holes are 1/8 in larger than the nominal bolt. They straddle the vertical and horizontal centrelines rather than sitting on them, so hole spacing round the circle is 3.02 in.

What pressure is an NPS 12 Class 600 flange good for?

1480 psig (102.0 bar) at 100 °F in A105 carbon steel, falling as metal temperature rises. Class 600 is a designation, not a pressure — see the P-T tables for your material and temperature.

Is a Class 600 weld neck flange interchangeable with another type?

At the bolted joint, yes: every B16.5 type in NPS 12 Class 600 shares the 22.00 in outside diameter, 19.25 in bolt circle and 20 × 1 1/4 in bolting, so any two will mate. What is not interchangeable is how they join the pipe, and therefore what service they suit.
