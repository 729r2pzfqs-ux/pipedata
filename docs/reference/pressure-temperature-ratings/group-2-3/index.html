Group 2.3 Pressure-Temperature Ratings

Low-carbon (L-grade) austenitic stainless steels, including 317L. The lower carbon buys weld-decay resistance at the cost of a slightly lower allowable stress than the standard grades. ASME B16.5 does not publish ratings for this group above 850 °F.

Common questions

Which materials are in ASME B16.5 Group 2.3?

A182 F304L, A182 F316L, A182 F317L, A240 304L/316L, A312 TP304L, A312 TP316L, A312 TP317L.

What is a Class 300 Group 2.3 flange rated at 600 °F?

370 psig (25.5 bar). At 100 °F the same flange is rated 600 psig (41.4 bar).
