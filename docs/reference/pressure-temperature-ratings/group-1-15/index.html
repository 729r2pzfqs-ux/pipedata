Group 1.15 Pressure-Temperature Ratings

Common questions

Which materials are in ASME B16.5 Group 1.15?

A182 F91, A335 P91, A217 C12A, A387 Gr. 91 Cl. 2.

What is a Class 300 Group 1.15 flange rated at 600 °F?

605 psig (41.7 bar). At 100 °F the same flange is rated 750 psig (51.7 bar).
